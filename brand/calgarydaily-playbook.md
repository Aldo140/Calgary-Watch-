# @calgarydaily playbook

The posting agent reads this file before it plans the week and before it
writes every post. Change anything here in plain words and the next run
follows it. Aldo owns this file.

## Who we are for

Calgarians aged roughly 20 to 40 who want to know what's happening in their
city and what to do with their evening or weekend. They follow us because we
show them Calgary, not because we list events.

## What the numbers say (Oct 2026, from our own account)

- Credited Reels of real Calgary moments (shared with the creator's
  permission) got a median of 2,500 views and 248 likes. Our best got 114,000.
- Text cards listing events got a median of 42 views and 0 likes. Instagram
  barely shows them to anyone.
- Posts with a real photo or video of a place or a moment do far better than
  anything with only words on it.
- Posts from 3 to 6 pm and after 9 pm did best; 6 to 9 pm and Sundays were
  the weakest.

## Rules for every post

1. **Show something.** Every post needs a real photo or video: the event's own
   photo, a place, a creator's clip shared with permission, or one of ours.
   A post that is only words on a card is the last resort.
2. **The first line is the hook.** It has to make a Calgarian stop scrolling:
   a specific surprise, a useful "you can do this tonight", a local
   reference, a number. Plain words, sentence case, no clickbait, no lies.
   Good: "Free skating at the Olympic Oval tonight, and it's their birthday."
   Bad: "Here's what's happening in Calgary today."
3. **Pick, don't list.** One great thing beats five okay ones. A roundup is
   for Today, Tonight and the weekend only.
4. **Give a reason to save or share.** Say why someone would send this to a
   friend: it's free, it's this weekend only, it's a good date, it's a
   cheap night out.
5. **End with one natural question when it fits** ("Who's going?", "Best
   patio in Kensington, go."). Never "tag a friend", "like if", or
   "comment below".
6. **Facts are sacred.** Never invent times, prices, quotes or what a place
   feels like. If we don't know it, we don't say it.
7. **Credit always.** Name the organizer or photographer. Other people's
   videos only with their written yes (see brand/reposts/README.md).

## Content mix for a week (up to 5 posts a day)

- **Things to do (40%)**: one standout event with its photo, Tonight and
  weekend Reels with a photo per slide, date night ideas, free stuff.
- **Calgary moments (30%)**: creator Reels shared with permission: weather,
  wildlife, skylines, Stampede, Flames and Stamps game-day energy, chinooks.
  These grew the account. The agent drafts the DM asking for permission;
  Aldo sends it.
- **Useful city news (20%)**: road closures, new openings, city decisions that
  change someone's week. Credible sources only.
- **Local and relatable (10%)**: Calgary inside jokes, "only in Calgary",
  neighbourhood pride. Original, never a copied meme.

## Never

- Engagement bait ("tag a friend", "like if", "comment YES").
- Hype words (epic, iconic, unmissable, must-see, hidden gem, vibes).
- Crime, crashes or tragedy as entertainment.
- Reposting anyone's photo or video without their permission.
- Auto-commenting on other accounts or sending cold DMs from the bot.
