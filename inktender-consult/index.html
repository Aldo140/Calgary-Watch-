How it works

Three steps.
Zero guesswork.

Most consults burn the first twenty minutes figuring out what you want. Do that part here, at your own pace, and spend the consult on the design.

  1. 01

    Tell us the idea

    Rough is fine. What it is, the feeling, the story behind it. Starters help if you blank.

  2. 02

    Mark the spot

    Tap the body map, slide to the size, snap a photo of the area and drop in your references.

  3. 03

    Sit down ready

    Your sheet prints at the studio before you arrive. Your artist has already been thinking about it.
