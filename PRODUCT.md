# CalgaryWatch

## Purpose
The place to find out what?s happening in Calgary: events, markets, local businesses, food, guides and neighbourhoods alongside live community awareness.

## Users
Calgary residents, visitors, commuters, local organizers and businesses. Discovery helps people plan their day; Live helps them understand recent reports nearby.

## Product modes
**Discovery:** clean editorial browsing, strong search, source-attributed entities, useful dates and local context.
**CalgaryWatch Live:** the existing /map application for crime, traffic, weather, infrastructure, outages and community reports. Its exact proposition remains **See it. Share it. Calgary knows.**

## Principles
- Preserve the working Firebase, authentication, ingestion, moderation, analytics, email and focused safety SEO infrastructure.
- Build incremental vertical slices. The foundation milestone is documented in docs/v2-implementation.md.
- Never invent live activity, availability, source verification or editorial rankings.
- Show provenance, timestamps, uncertainty and sponsorship where they affect interpretation.
- Keep paid visibility independent of editorial selection. Free for residents; do not assert an unverified legal organizational status.
- Protect existing administrator access until a replacement Firebase identity has been authenticated and tested.
- Do not imply police affiliation, dispatch access or official endorsement. Emergency actions remain unmistakable; CalgaryWatch is not a substitute for 911.
- Avoid thin keyword pages, fear-driven neighbourhood rankings and identifying private individuals.

## Design
The Nudge informs hero clarity; The Infatuation informs editorial hierarchy. CalgaryWatch retains its own identity. See docs/design-system.md. WCAG 2.2 AA is the target: keyboard, screen readers, reduced motion, 200% zoom and touch support.
