## Discovery improvement implemented

Tourism Calgary discovery previously stopped at 60 days; on October 16 that excludes the latter part of December. The mapper and fetch filter now share a bounded 90-day horizon. A regression test checks that Christmas Eve and New Year’s Eve are included while a January 15 event remains outside the window. Ticketmaster already uses 90 days; it was not extended or given a larger request budget.

This prepares the next successful ingestion to find later dates. It does not claim a feed ran, that dates were verified or that a new event was published. The existing source/page limits remain in place.
